# City Crooks
Build your empire. Rule the streets. Stay one step ahead.
City Crooks is a single-player, browser-based crime strategy RPG. Start
small, complete jobs, grow your cash, improve your character, and unlock
tougher territories as you level up.
Features
- Run jobs: Take on street jobs and unlock higher-risk operations
  as you progress.
- Manage resources: Keep an eye on cash, energy, and heat.
- Train your character: Spend cash and energy to improve Strength,
  Defence, Agility, or Intel.
- Trade contraband: Risk money for a chance to make a profit.
- Lay low: Recover energy and reduce heat.
- Unlock territories: Progress through 15 districts, from Downtown
  to The Summit.
- Buy black-market tools: Purchase specialist toolkit items for
  bonuses during your current level.
- Progress through ranks: Earn XP, level up, and take on more
  challenging operations.
How to Play
1. Open the game website in a modern browser.
2. Start in Downtown with your initial cash and energy.
3. Select an operation and check its costs and risks before starting.
4. Complete jobs and training to earn XP and increase your level.
5. Use the territory selector to move into new districts when you meet
   their level requirements.
6. Manage your heat and energy. If energy is low, Lay Low to
   recover some energy and reduce heat.
7. Spend carefully: some operations have setup costs, and failed jobs
   can cost you money.
Resources
- Cash: Used to train, trade, and pay operation setup costs.
- Energy: Most operations consume energy. You can recover energy
  by laying low.
- Heat: Risky activity increases heat. Higher heat can make
  operations more dangerous.
- XP and Level: Earn XP from activities to unlock new territories
  and jobs.
- Character stats: Strength, Defence, Agility, and Intel improve
  as you train.
Territories
The game includes 15 territories with increasing level requirements:
1. Downtown
2. The Docks
3. Old Quarter
4. Financial Row
5. Cargo Terminal
6. Diamond District
7. Harbor Heights
8. Crown Heights
9. Blackstone Ward
10. Neon Mile
11. Tech Quarter
12. Museum Mile
13. Embassy Row
14. The Summit
Black-Market Toolkit
Toolkit items provide bonuses such as reduced job failure risk, smaller
losses on failed jobs, reduced heat, or extra maximum energy. Tool
ownership resets when you level up, so plan purchases around your
current level.
Hosting on GitHub Pages
This game is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript website and can be hosted
using GitHub Pages.
1. Rename the game file to index.html.
2. Create a public repository on GitHub, for example city-crooks.
3. Upload index.html to the repository root and commit the file.
4. Open Settings → Pages.
5. Under the build and deployment settings, select Deploy from a
   branch, choose the main branch and / (root), then save.
6. After GitHub Pages publishes the site, share the URL shown in the
   Pages settings.
GitHub Pages offers free hosting for public repositories. A custom
domain is optional and may cost extra.
Notes
- City Crooks is a fictional game for entertainment.
- The current game is a single-player experience.
- Progress is currently held in the page while you play; it is not
  saved permanently between sessions.
- For the best experience, open the published website in a modern
  browser rather than using an email attachment preview.
